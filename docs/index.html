What is Anti-Palestinian Racism (APR)?

Anti-Palestinian Racism is a subset of anti-Arab racism. It includes any distinction, dehumanising language, silencing, stereotyping, defamation, exclusion, restriction, or preference that has the purpose or effect of denying Palestinians or their supporters equal rights, fundamental freedoms, or personal security.

This description draws on Article 1 of the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination, and on the Arab Canadian Lawyers Association’s description of Anti-Palestinian Racism.

ICERD provides the general racial-discrimination framework. The Arab Canadian Lawyers Association provides the specific Anti-Palestinian Racism framing.

In February 2026, the UK’s Palestine Solidarity Campaign AGM formally recognised 18 non-exhaustive examples of anti-Palestinian racism. This page presents those examples in a clear, structured and accessible format for readers, researchers, educators and campaigners.

The 18 PSC-adopted examples of Anti-Palestinian Racism

The summaries below make the PSC-recognised examples visible in crawlable HTML. For the authoritative wording, see PSC’s motions document linked in the provenance section.

Erasure and denial of ethnic or indigenous identity

  • 1. Denying Palestinian indigeneity in historic Palestine. (harassment; interference with identity; dehumanisation)
  • 2. Denying the Nakba. (harassment; restriction on expression; denialism)
  • 3. Failing to acknowledge Palestinians as an indigenous people with collective identity, belonging, and rights in historic Palestine. (direct discrimination; interference with identity)

Dehumanisation creating a hostile environment

  • 4. Erasing Palestinians’ human rights, equal dignity, and worth. (direct discrimination; dehumanisation; harassment; interference with identity)

Racial stereotyping, criminalisation and defamations

  • 5. Defaming Palestinians or allies as inherently antisemitic, terrorist-linked, terrorist-sympathetic, or opposed to democratic values. (direct discrimination; harassment; restriction on expression)
  • 6. Justifying violence against Palestinians. (dehumanisation; direct discrimination; incitement to violence; failure to protect)

Exclusion and suppression of Palestinian participation

  • 7. Requiring Palestinians or supporters to denounce acts more strongly than others, or holding them collectively responsible for others’ acts. (direct discrimination; harassment; restriction on expression)
  • 8. Excluding Palestinian perspectives, Palestinians, or allies, or pressuring others to exclude them. (direct discrimination; harassment; restriction on participation)
  • 9. Failing to engage with Palestinian victims of war crimes or racism while engaging with other racial groups. (indirect discrimination; failure to protect)

Unequal application of legal, political and humanitarian standards

  • 10. Denying, delaying, or undermining recognition of Palestine as a state while supporting recognition of Israel. (structural discrimination; impairment of collective rights; restriction on participation)
  • 11. Calling for a safe and secure Israel while omitting Palestinian safety and security. (direct discrimination; failure to protect; humanitarian neglect)
  • 12. Denying Palestinian refugees and descendants the right of return under international law. (direct discrimination; exclusion)
  • 13. Not applying the same international law standards to Palestinians as to other groups. (direct discrimination; restriction on participation)

Systemic and institutionalised racial discrimination

  • 14. Supporting, denying, or minimising systems of apartheid imposed on Palestinians. (apartheid; apartheid denial; structural discrimination)
  • 15. Promoting or excusing extremist settler ideology, anti-Palestinian hate or violence, discriminatory laws, or theft of Palestinian land and resources. (direct discrimination; structural violence and discrimination; dispossession/theft)
  • 16. Recognising the discriminatory and dispossessive impact of privileging one group over indigenous Palestinians in the Balfour Declaration. (direct discrimination; dispossession)

Intersection with Islamophobia

  • 17. Islamophobia and hatred targeting Palestinian Muslims, Palestinians perceived as Muslim, or Muslims supporting Palestinian rights. (direct discrimination; racialisation)

Targeting supporters of Palestinian rights by association

  • 18. Targeting, vilifying, or penalising people for solidarity with Palestinians or support for Palestinian rights. (direct discrimination; harassment; restriction on expression; racialisation by association)
